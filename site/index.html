Scope

This shows the reasons given in the decisions we hold, not every solar-on-farmland permit in Oregon.

  • County records come from a public records request and are approved decisions only.
  • The only county denials are two of three hand-collected LUBA sample cases; the third is a county approval that LUBA reversed.
  • We do not claim a final outcome. Appeals and court decisions we did not collect are missing.
  • LUBA cases are reported separately and never pooled with county decisions.
