# Oregon Farmland Solar: design handoff

Read in this order:

1. `handoff.md`: build brief (files, behaviours, data states, data contract).
2. `README.md`: style guide (colours, type, spacing, components, content rules).
3. `accessibility.md`: decisions and open questions. `contrast.md`: every contrast ratio.
4. `assets/Handoff/`: `site-tokens.css`, `site.css` and `tokens.dtcg.json` to copy into the site.
5. `previews/*.html`: open in a browser. `Screen*.html` and `DataModes.html` are the screens (desktop and phone, light and dark); the rest are components. Standalone, no network.

All numbers, counties and quotes in the previews are placeholders or invented. The county map shapes are mockup geometry (Census, generalized): use the BLM boundaries from the project data instead.
