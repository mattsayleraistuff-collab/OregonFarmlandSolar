Below is one page of a Land Use Board of Appeals opinion. Find each distinct ground the
decision-maker (the county or LUBA) gave for its decision on this page.

A reason is a stated ground that explains WHY the decision-maker approved, denied,
affirmed, reversed or remanded. Only include a ground the decision-maker adopts or
relies on. Do not include testimony or arguments the decision-maker merely reports
without a finding.

Do not return: the outcome or disposition line (e.g. "AFFIRMED", "REVERSED",
"Final Opinion and Order"), captions, party or counsel lists, dates, or procedural
history.

Output only a JSON object in this form, nothing else:
{"has_reasons": true or false, "reasons": [{"quote": "...", "source": "county" or "LUBA"}]}

Rules:
- "quote" must be copied word for word from the page text, under 40 words. Never write
  words that are not on the page. If a sentence is cut off at the end of the page, quote
  only the part that is on this page.
- "source" is "county" if the ground is the county's, "LUBA" if it is the Board's.
- If the page contains only a caption, disposition, party or counsel list, or procedural
  history, output {"has_reasons": false, "reasons": []}.
- One entry per distinct ground, not one per sentence.
